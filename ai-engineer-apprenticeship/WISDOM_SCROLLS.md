# Wisdom Scrolls

Principles earned through practice. Add a scroll only when experience makes it real.

## Scroll 1

> Never ask AI to build something you could not debug tomorrow.

## Scroll 2

> Shipping teaches the product. Understanding teaches the engineer.

## Scroll 3

> A passing test is evidence, not absolution.

## Scroll 4

> When three prompts fail, stop spinning the crank and reduce the problem.

## Scroll 5

> Unknown code is borrowed complexity with interest.

## Scroll 6

> One completed loop restores more confidence than five looming projects.

## Scroll 7

> Measure quality on held-out examples, not on the demonstration that inspired the design.

## Scroll 8

> Logs are evidence. Commands are questions. Debugging is the conversation between them.

## Scroll 9

> Speed is useful only when direction and ownership survive it.

## Scroll 10

> Recovery is part of the production system. A permanently activated engineer is a degrading dependency.
